About me

A draftsman’s eye for detail, an engineer’s respect for standards.

I am Zarif Hossain, a patent and technical drawing specialist. I turn rough sketches, photos and prototypes into clean, standards-compliant drawing sheets — the kind patent attorneys file and workshops build from without questions.

Most of my work has come through Fiverr, where I have completed 550+ projects as Zarif Hossain for inventors, startups and law firms — from first-sketch utility figures to full production drawing packages.
